# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Individuals, professionals, and students who time-box their days and want an intuitive, tactile daily planner. They need to visualize their schedule in a calendar and forge their day hour-by-hour by dragging activities onto a visual timeline.

## Product Purpose

DayForge bridges high-level calendar scheduling (month view) and granular execution (half-hour timeline day view). It makes daily time-blocking fast, flexible, and satisfying through direct drag-and-drop manipulation and reusable activity templates.

## Positioning

Unlike bloated enterprise calendar suites or rigid to-do checklists, DayForge treats time as visual blocks you drag, snap, and resize onto your day. It combines tactile activity templates with real-time planning, cloud auth, and offline resilience.

## Tone & Experience Principles

- **Craft over decoration**: Clean, typography-first, functional interface. No AI design slop (no heavy side-tabs, no decorative gradients, no kicker eyebrows, no emojis as UI chrome).
- **Tactile & Responsive**: High-feedback interactions: smooth drag-and-drop, intuitive resizing, clear hover cues, and accessible keyboard states.
- **Calm & Focused**: Thoughtfully restrained palette with semantic color coding for activity categories. High contrast, readable typography, and clean information hierarchy.
